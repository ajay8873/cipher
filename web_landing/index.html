Engineered for Precision & Absolute Privacy

Automated ledger bookkeeping without sacrificing control over your personal financial data.

Bank SMS Parsing

Extracts amount, merchant entity, date, and debit/credit status from incoming bank SMS streams using high-precision local regex engines.

UPI Notification Listener

Captures real-time credit notifications from PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, and Navi when bank SMS triggers are delayed or dropped by carriers.

Hybrid Deduplication

Cross-references SMS logs and notification payloads in a 5-minute rolling window (±₹0.05) to ensure exact single-entry recording.

100% Local Storage

All transaction history remains strictly local in an isolated Android SQLite database. No telemetry, no ads, and zero account sign-in required.

Interactive Analytics

Categorized spending distribution donut charts, today's credit/debit summary cards, and quick daily/monthly date range filtering.

DeepSeek AI Engine

Optional AI parsing engine fallback using DeepSeek API for non-standard bank transaction formats and unusual merchant descriptions.

Your Money Data Belongs Only to You

Cipher operates with complete offline autonomy. No remote analytics telemetry, no cloud backend requirements, and no background tracking.
